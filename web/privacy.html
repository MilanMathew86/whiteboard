Privacy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

  • You can use ChalkBack without an account. Your board then stays on your device, and in your own Google Drive only if you save it there.
  • An account is optional. If you sign in with Google, ChalkBack's server stores your name, email address, profile picture and the boards you save to your account, so you can open them on other devices and share them.
  • Boards you share are visible only to the people you share them with. ChalkBack's admins can see account details, but not your boards.
  • You can download everything in your account, or delete your account and all your boards, at any time.
  • Google Drive access is limited to the files ChalkBack creates or that you pick yourself.
  • AI features are optional. When you use one, only the strokes you selected are sent to Anthropic for processing, and you're told so first.
  • A Claude API key, if you add one, stays in your browser: for the current tab only, unless you choose to remember it.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

Where your boards live

Drawing, undo, replay and PDF export all happen in your browser. Where a board is stored depends on how you use ChalkBack:

  • Signed out, or using the board on this device: the board is stored only in your browser. ChalkBack's server never receives it.
  • Boards in your account: saved on ChalkBack's server as you draw, so you can open them on your other devices and share them.
  • Google Drive: only when you choose to save there (see below).

Your ChalkBack account

You sign in with your Google account; ChalkBack never sees or stores a password. At sign-in Google tells ChalkBack your Google account ID, name, email address (only if Google has verified it) and profile picture. The server stores these, plus:

  • a display name, if you set one,
  • the boards you save to your account: drawings, images you add, the board's style, and the undo/replay history with the time of each action,
  • who you've shared each board with (their email address) and whether they can view or edit it,
  • when you joined and when you last signed in.

ChalkBack uses Google only to confirm who you are when you sign in. It doesn't keep Google's sign-in tokens, and signing in to your account gives ChalkBack no access to your Google Drive or any other Google data.

Staying signed in uses one cookie, which holds a signed reference to your account. It's sent only to ChalkBack, can't be read by the page's scripts, and lasts 30 days unless you sign out. Sign out of all devices (in Settings) ends every session at once.

Sharing

When you share a board, you choose an email address and whether that person can view or edit it. ChalkBack doesn't email anyone; you send the link yourself. The link only opens the board for people it's shared with, once they've signed in with that email address. People you share with see your display name (or your Google name) and email address as the board's owner. Anyone who can edit a board can change its content and name.

Admins

ChalkBack's administrators can see each account's name, email address, role, status, number of boards, storage used, and when it joined and last signed in. They can disable, enable or delete accounts and sign them out. They can't see your boards' titles or content, unless you share a board with them like anyone else. Admin actions are recorded in a log.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your account and boards are kept until you delete them. In Settings you can:

  • Download my data: a ZIP file with every board you own (in the same format as a Google Drive save) and your account details, including who you've shared boards with.
  • Delete account: deletes your account, every board you own and the shares on them, and any shares to your email address, straight away. Boards other people shared with you aren't affected. A log entry records that an account was deleted, without your name or email address.

Deleting a board deletes it for everyone it was shared with. An admin can also delete an account, with the same effect.

What's stored on your device

ChalkBack keeps the following in your browser's storage:

  • Your board (localStorage): your drawings, images you add, the view position, and the undo/replay history with the time of each action, so your work survives a page refresh.
  • Unsaved changes to account boards (localStorage): if a change can't reach the server (for example while you're offline), a copy is kept until it's saved, so it isn't lost.
  • Your Claude API key, if you add one: in sessionStorage by default, which your browser deletes when you close the tab, or in localStorage if you tick Remember key on this device (see below).
  • Whether you've seen the AI notice (localStorage), so it's only shown once.

The board on your device never leaves it unless you save it to your account or Google Drive, or use an AI feature. To delete this data, open Settings and choose Clear local data, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Clearing local data also signs you out; it doesn't affect boards in your account or files you saved to Google Drive.

Google Drive

Saving to and opening from Google Drive is optional and separate from your ChalkBack account. When you use it, you sign in with Google and grant ChalkBack the drive.file permission. That permission only lets ChalkBack access:

  • files that ChalkBack itself created in your Drive, and
  • files you explicitly choose in the Google file picker.

ChalkBack cannot see, list, read or change any other files in your Drive, and Drive access doesn't give it your contacts or any other Google account data.

The Drive token Google issues is kept only in the page's memory while it's open, and is discarded when you close or reload the page. Files are sent directly between your browser and Google; they never pass through ChalkBack's server. Sign out of Drive in Settings revokes the token with Google straight away. You can also remove ChalkBack's access at any time from your Google Account connections page.

ChalkBack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI processing (Claude)

ChalkBack's AI features, Solve Math and Clean Diagram, are optional and use Anthropic's Claude. When you use one, your browser renders only the strokes you selected as an image and sends it, with your API key, straight to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) for processing. Nothing else on your board is sent, and nothing passes through a ChalkBack server. The first time you use an AI feature, ChalkBack shows a notice saying that your selected drawing is sent to Anthropic, and nothing is sent unless you continue.

Anthropic processes these requests under its own privacy policy and the terms of your API account.

Claude API key

AI features use your own Anthropic API key, which you add in Settings. By default the key is kept in sessionStorage and is deleted when you close the tab. If you tick Remember key on this device, it's kept in localStorage until you choose Forget key or Clear local data. The key is never included in your board, your exports or your Drive files, and it's never sent anywhere except directly to api.anthropic.com.

Anyone with access to your browser profile could read a stored key, so don't remember a key on a shared computer, and consider using a dedicated key with a spending limit.

Third-party services

To provide its features, ChalkBack's pages load code from a few outside services. Like any website, these services receive standard request details such as your IP address and browser type:

  • Our hosting provider runs ChalkBack's server and database, which store account boards and account details as described above.
  • Google provides sign-in, the file picker and Drive storage (Google privacy policy).
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the jsPDF library used for PDF export (Cloudflare privacy policy).
  • Anthropic, only when you use a Claude feature with your own key, as described above.

ChalkBack sets only the sign-in cookie described above, and uses no analytics or advertising services. We don't sell or rent your information, or use it for anything other than running ChalkBack.

Security

ChalkBack is served only over HTTPS, and its pages use a Content Security Policy that only allows the services listed above. The PDF library is pinned to an exact version and checked against a fixed integrity hash. Every board, whether opened from your account, a share, Google Drive or your browser, is checked strictly before use: anything other than ChalkBack's own board format, including images that point to outside web addresses, is rejected. The server checks on every request that you're allowed to see or change the board you ask for.

Children

ChalkBack doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us (below) and we'll delete it.
