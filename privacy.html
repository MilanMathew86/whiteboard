Privacy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

  • ChalkBack runs entirely in your browser. There is no ChalkBack server, account or database.
  • Your drawings are stored on your device, and in your own Google Drive only if you choose to save them there.
  • Google Drive access is limited to the files ChalkBack creates or that you pick yourself.
  • A Claude API key, if you add one, stays in your browser.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

The app runs in your browser

ChalkBack is a set of static files (HTML, CSS and JavaScript) served by GitHub Pages. Everything you do, including drawing, undo, replay and PDF export, happens on your own device. We don't operate any server that receives, stores or processes your drawings or personal information.

What's stored on your device

ChalkBack uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) for:

  • Your board: your drawings, images you add, the view position, and the undo/replay history with the time of each action, so your work survives a page refresh.
  • Your Claude API key, if you add one (see below).

This data never leaves your device unless you save to Google Drive or use a Claude feature. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Google Drive

Saving to and opening from Google Drive is optional. When you use it, you sign in with Google and grant ChalkBack the drive.file permission. That permission only lets ChalkBack access:

  • files that ChalkBack itself created in your Drive, and
  • files you explicitly choose in the Google file picker.

ChalkBack cannot see, list, read or change any other files in your Drive. It does not access your name, email address, contacts or any other Google account data.

The sign-in token Google issues is kept only in the page's memory while it's open, and is discarded when you close or reload the page. Files are sent directly between your browser and Google; they never pass through any ChalkBack server. You can remove ChalkBack's access at any time from your Google Account connections page.

ChalkBack's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Claude API key

ChalkBack's AI features (Solve Math and Clean Diagram) use your own Anthropic API key, which you can add in Settings. The key is stored only in your browser's local storage. It is never included in your board, your exports or your Drive files, and it is never sent anywhere except directly to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com).

When you use one of these features, your browser sends an image of only the strokes you selected, together with your key, straight to Anthropic. Anthropic handles that request under its own privacy policy. You can remove the key at any time with Remove key in Settings.

Anyone with access to your browser profile could read a stored key, so don't add one on a shared computer.

Third-party services

To provide its features, ChalkBack's pages load code from a few outside services. Like any website, these services receive standard request details such as your IP address and browser type:

  • GitHub Pages hosts ChalkBack's files (GitHub privacy statement).
  • Google provides sign-in, the file picker and Drive storage (Google privacy policy).
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the jsPDF library used for PDF export (Cloudflare privacy policy).
  • Anthropic, only when you use a Claude feature with your own key, as described above.

ChalkBack itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising services.

Children

ChalkBack doesn't knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
